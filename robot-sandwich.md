# Robot Sandwich M1_T
# 08/27/2026
# CTS-285
# Brandon Malave 


###Assumptions 
- Robot is powered up and has 2 functional arms 
- There is counter space 
- Butter Knife is provided and placed on counter 
- Bread and Peanut Butter are provided and placed on counter as well

###Steps 
- Grab two slices of bread and place them down on the counter 
- Pick up Peanut Butter jar, twist open, then place down on the counter 
- Grab Butter Knife in one hand
- Insert butter knife in peanut butter to obtain peanut butter on butter knife 
- Pull the knife out and spread peanut butter on the both slices of bread evenly
- put butter knife down 
- pick up both slices of bread and join them together 


###Done When
-Bread with Peanut Butter us put together and placed on counter.
